      About Whole Truth Maps

      Every pixel here is drawn by code, not copied from anywhere. The city is squeezed so that each landmark gets a neighbourhood, so treat distances as a story and not a survey.

      The truth is the checkable part: dates, names, numbers. The whole truth is the lived part: what people actually do there, what is changing, and how the under-25s talk about it. That half is opinion, written with affection.

      Green is the Garden City the plaques describe. Pink is the pub city at sunset, when the sky does that thing.

      Whole Truth Maps is made by Ginhouse at ginclair.com/ginhouse.
